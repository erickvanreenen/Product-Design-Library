# Write the design rationale

Turns the decision log into a clear, honest case for a design, shaped for the audience who will read or hear it.

## Steps
1. **State the decision** specifically.
2. **Give context:** the problem, constraints and goal.
3. **Show the options considered** and why they lost.
4. **Present the evidence:** research, data, tests, principles. Separate evidence from craft judgement and say which is which.
5. **Name the trade-offs** and who they affect.
6. **Explain how it will be validated:** the metrics and the review date.
7. **Shape for the audience:** executives get impact and the ask first; engineers get detail and edge cases; designers get process and system fit. For a presentation use hook, context, journey, solution, evidence, ask, with one idea per slide.
8. **Make the ask explicit:** approval, feedback or resources.

## Guardrails
- Write rationale while deciding, not afterwards.
- Do not present assumptions as findings.

## Output
A rationale document or deck outline.

## Done when
- A reader who was not in the room understands what was chosen, why, and what it costs.
