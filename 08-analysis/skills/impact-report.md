# Report design impact

Tells stakeholders what the design changed, with honest attribution and a clear next step.

## Steps
1. **Restate the outcome and targets** from the plan.
2. **Report in three layers:** user metrics (task success, errors, satisfaction), product metrics (conversion, onboarding completion, support contacts for the designed flows, accessibility score), and business metrics (revenue, churn, cost saved).
3. **Attribute honestly:** use experiment results where they exist; where design's share cannot be isolated, say the outcome is shared and name the other factors.
4. **Pair numbers with evidence from people:** quotes or observations that explain the change.
5. **Include what did not work** and what was learned.
6. **Recommend the next iteration:** what to tackle next and why (gate G9).
7. Lead with impact for executives; add detail for product and engineering audiences.

## Guardrails
- Do not claim credit for shared outcomes; do not hide misses.

## Output
A one-page summary (outcome, results against targets, attribution, learning, next step) with an appendix of data.

## Done when
- Each result is compared with its target, attribution is stated, and the next iteration is named.
