# Research

What is true about users, their domain and the market, with confidence marked.

**Position in the line:** step 1 of `playbooks/run-brief.md`.  
**Writes:** work/01-research.md, research and glossary sections of UX.md  
**Gate:** G1 Evidence

## Running this stage alone

Needs only a short brief: the decision the research serves and by when. Existing notes or transcripts go to `synthesise-findings` first.

## Skills, in running order

| # | Skill | Step | Use when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Plan the research](skills/research-plan.md) | 1 | Starting research; "what should we find out", "plan a study", "do we need interviews or a survey"; the brief's bottleneck is observed behaviour or user needs. |
| 2 | [Write an interview guide](skills/interview-guide.md) | 1 | Interviews are planned; "write the interview script", "what should we ask users". |
| 3 | [Synthesise findings](skills/synthesise-findings.md) | 1 | Research material exists (transcripts, notes, tickets, survey results, analytics); "make sense of this research", "what did we learn", "summarise these interviews". |
| 4 | [Define needs and jobs](skills/needs-and-jobs.md) | 1 | After synthesis; "what are users trying to do", "write job stories", "what are the needs and pains". |
| 5 | [Build inclusive personas](skills/inclusive-personas.md) | 1 | The team needs a shared reference for who it designs for; "create personas"; before UX work on a product with several user types. |
| 6 | [Map the journey](skills/journey-map.md) | 1 | Improving an existing experience; "map the journey", "where does it go wrong end to end"; an experience spans several channels or involves staff. |
| 7 | [Scan the competition](skills/competitive-scan.md) | 1 | Entering a market or redesigning a known pattern; "what do others do", "benchmark this". |
| 8 | [Map the domain and glossary](skills/domain-glossary.md) | 1 | The domain is complex or unfamiliar; teams or users use different words for the same thing; before the conceptual model. |

## Gate checklist: G1 Evidence (Research)

- [ ] Each finding links to raw evidence
- [ ] Confidence stated for each finding
- [ ] Assumptions separated from findings
- [ ] Gaps listed
