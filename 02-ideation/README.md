# Ideation and strategy

Which problem to solve, which bets to place, and which direction to take, chosen on criteria set in advance.

**Position in the line:** step 2 of `playbooks/run-brief.md`.  
**Writes:** work/02-direction.md  
**Gate:** G2 Direction

## Running this stage alone

Needs a problem and some evidence. Without research, mark every opportunity as assumed and plan the cheapest test.

## Skills, in running order

| # | Skill | Step | Use when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Frame the problem](skills/frame-problem.md) | 2 | Research is done and the team must choose what to solve; "which problem do we tackle", "how might we", "what is the opportunity". |
| 2 | [Place bets and test assumptions](skills/assumptions-and-bets.md) | 2 | A problem is framed; "what could we build", "what is the riskiest assumption", "how do we test this cheaply". |
| 3 | [Set design principles](skills/design-principles.md) | 2 | The same trade-offs keep being argued; a new product or major redesign starts; "what are our principles", "what is the vision". |
| 4 | [Explore concepts in parallel](skills/explore-concepts.md) | 2 | A problem is framed and there is one idea on the table (or none); "give me options", "explore directions", "sketch some concepts". |
| 5 | [Select a concept](skills/select-concept.md) | 2 | Concepts exist and a direction must be chosen; "which one do we go with", "help us decide". |

## Gate checklist: G2 Direction (Ideation)

- [ ] More than one concept explored
- [ ] Criteria fixed before scoring
- [ ] One direction chosen by a person
- [ ] Success metrics defined
