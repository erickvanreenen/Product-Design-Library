# Run a critique session

Runs a structured critique with people that produces clear, actionable decisions instead of opinions.

## Not for
- One expert reviewing against heuristics, use `heuristic-review`.

## Steps
1. **Before:** the designer shares goals, constraints, audience, stage of work and exactly what feedback is wanted. Share AI critique findings in advance so the session focuses on judgement.
2. **Present** (about 5 minutes): the work and the problem it solves.
3. **Clarify** (about 5 minutes): questions to understand, not to judge.
4. **Feedback rounds** by area or priority, using four forms: "I notice..." (observation), "I wonder..." (question), "What if..." (alternative), "I think... because..." (opinion with a reason tied to users, principles or evidence).
5. **Discuss** the main tensions.
6. **Capture** decisions and actions with owners and dates. The designer summarises at the end.
7. For each point, record accepted or rejected with a reason (gate G7).

## Guardrails
- Feedback refers to user needs, principles and evidence, not personal preference.
- The session advises; the named decider decides. Avoid design by committee.

## Output
Decisions, accepted and rejected points with reasons, actions with owners.

## Done when
- Every point is accepted or rejected with a reason, and actions have owners.
